Delivery-tree costs now follow the game's tiers from bottom (Tier 2/3) to top (Tier 9), and Faxit disks gain capacity through twenty new Extra Disk Bytes upgrades.

**Delivery tree costs**

- Every task's cost now matches its height in the tree: steel parts at the bottom, motors and
  encased beams next, then oil parts, computers, aluminium, nuclear/turbo parts, and quantum parts
  at the very top. No more Tier 7/8 parts on a task that sits two steps above Nexus Capacity 2.
- The same rule applies to every branch — Nexus Capacity, all Calibration lines, Solid/Fluid
  Transfer speeds, Delivery Queue, Depot Upload Speed, Nexus Expansion and the Disk Bytes upgrades —
  so parallel branches at the same height ask for parts of the same tier.
- Faxit parts scale along too: Data Cables and Polarization Frames early, Poled Kerr Crystals in the
  middle, Photonic Processors only in the upper tree.
- Faxit building and part recipes follow the same ladder: Input/Output ports use Rotors and Steel
  Beams, Delivery Port and Terminal use Motors and Encased Industrial Beams, the Radar Tower uses
  Circuit Boards and Plastic, the Mk2–Mk4 disks use Crystal Oscillators, Computers, AI Limiters and
  Heavy Modular Frames. The Photonic Processor needs Crystal Oscillators instead of Time Crystals.
- Somersloop and Mercer Recovery deliveries no longer ask for ingots.
- Every task now shows the icon of the part it asks for (Recovery tasks their Somersloop/Mercer reward,
  Recycle tasks the part they recycle) — the tier ladder is visible at a glance.
- Project-Part Recycling gates now cost 100 Project Parts for Phase 1 and 100 more per phase
  (200, 300, 400, 500), split evenly across that phase's parts.
- The Faxit Steel Foundation no longer asks for Faxit parts it unlocks itself. Somersloop/Mercer slot
  upgrades, AWESOME Sink coupon costs and Hazardous Logistics are unchanged.

**Disk capacity**

- Added five optional upgrades after each Small, Medium, Large, and Huge disk unlock.
- Every upgrade adds 20% of base byte capacity to all disks. Bonuses add: +100% per
  five-upgrade branch, +400% after all twenty upgrades.
- Each upgrade costs a different set of five items from its tier in the tree, with varied
  quantities of 250–1,000 per item.
- All four established disk types and their crafting recipes remain available.
  Additional capacity comes from upgrades rather than new disk variants.
- Existing byte allocations, disk rewards, calibration tasks, drive slots, and
  progression prerequisites are preserved. Extra bytes are available to allocate
  in the Storage Nexus.
